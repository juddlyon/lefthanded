<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is Ringo Starr left-handed?</h3>
<p>Yes. Ringo Starr is left-handed but plays a drum kit arranged for a right-hander. Leading fills with his left hand on a right-handed kit is a large part of what gives his drumming its distinctive feel.</p>

<h3>Is Danny Carey left-handed?</h3>
<p>Danny Carey of Tool appears on many lists of left-handed drummers, but the claim is unconfirmed. He plays a conventionally arranged kit with the hi-hat under his left foot, and we have not found an on-record statement from Carey about which hand he writes with.</p>

<h3>Is Josh Dun left-handed?</h3>
<p>Probably not. Josh Dun of Twenty One Pilots is often named on lists of left-handed drummers, but Wikipedia's list of left-handed musicians describes him as right-handed and notes that he frequently plays open-handed, leading with his left hand on the hi-hat.</p>

<h3>Is Phil Collins left-handed?</h3>
<p>Yes. Phil Collins is left-handed and plays a fully mirrored left-handed drum kit, with the hi-hat on his right and the ride cymbal on his left.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Which hand does a left-handed baseball glove go on?</h3>
<p>A left-handed thrower wears the glove on the right hand and throws with the left. Retailers call this a left hand throw glove, often shortened to LHT.</p>

<h3>What does left hand throw mean on a glove listing?</h3>
<p>Left hand throw means the glove is made for a player who throws with the left hand, so it fits the right hand. Right hand throw is the opposite and fits the left hand.</p>

<h3>What size baseball glove does a left-handed player need?</h3>
<p>Size depends on age and position, not on throwing hand. Youth gloves run from about 9 to 11.5 inches, and adult gloves from about 11.5 to 13 inches. Outfielders and slowpitch softball players use the larger sizes.</p>

<h3>Do they make left-handed catcher's mitts?</h3>
<p>Yes, but few models are sold in left hand throw because left-handed catchers are rare. The SSK Z5 Craftsman is one catcher's mitt that lists a left hand throw option.</p>

<h3>What positions do left-handed players usually play?</h3>
<p>Left-handed throwers most often play first base, the outfield, and pitcher. Second base, shortstop, third base, and catcher are harder for a left-hander because of the throwing angles to first base.</p>

<h2 id="what-counts-as-left-handed">What counts as left-handed</h2>

<p>A product earns a place in our guides in one of three ways:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Built left-handed.</strong> The maker produces a mirrored version, such as a left-handed guitar, golf club, or pair of scissors.</li>
<li><strong>Symmetrical.</strong> The product works the same in either hand, such as an ambidextrous mouse or a keyboard without a number pad.</li>
<li><strong>Solves a left-hander's problem.</strong> The product is not handed, but it fixes something lefties run into, such as fast-drying ink.</li>
</ul>

<p>Each guide says which of the three applies to each pick. We do not describe a right-handed product as left-handed.</p>

<h2 id="how-we-pick">How we pick</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>We confirm the left-handed version exists.</strong> We check the maker's specifications or the retail listing for a left-hand model or option.</li>
<li><strong>We check that it is in stock.</strong> Left-handed versions sell out and get discontinued more often than right-handed ones, so we favor products that are consistently available.</li>
<li><strong>We read the specifications.</strong> Our descriptions come from the maker's published details.</li>
<li><strong>We look at owner feedback.</strong> We favor products with a long record of buyer reviews over new listings with few.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-we-do-not-claim">What we do not claim</h2>

<p>We do not have a test lab, and we have not used every product we list. When a guide says a product is the best pick, that is our judgment from the research above, not the result of a hands-on test. If we have used a product ourselves, the guide says so.</p>

<p>We do not show prices, because they change and Amazon's terms do not allow us to display them. Each product button opens the current listing.</p>

<h2 id="links">How the links work</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>"Check Amazon Price"</strong> opens the exact product on Amazon.</li>
<li><strong>"Find It on Amazon"</strong> opens an Amazon search, used when we could not match one exact listing.</li>
<li><strong>"View at" a maker's name</strong> opens the maker's own site. We earn nothing from those links.</li>
</ul>

<p>Some Amazon listings sell the right-handed and left-handed versions on one page. Where that applies, the guide tells you to select the left-hand option before buying.</p>

<p>Amazon links are affiliate links. See our <a href="/disclosures/">disclosures</a> for how that works. Affiliate commissions do not decide which products we include.</p>

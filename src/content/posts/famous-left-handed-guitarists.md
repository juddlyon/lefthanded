<p><strong>Who are the greatest left-handed guitarists of all time?</strong> Jimi Hendrix, Paul McCartney, Kurt Cobain, Tony Iommi, Albert King, and Dick Dale all played guitar left-handed. Eric Clapton and Jimmy Page are often listed too, but both play right-handed.</p><p>This list only includes guitarists who are documented playing left-handed. Players who are often named as lefties without evidence are covered in a separate section near the end, along with a few natural left-handers who learned to play right-handed.</p><h2 id="jimi-hendrix">Jimi Hendrix</h2><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><img src="/content/images/2022/10/jimmy-hendrix-left-handed.jpg" class="kg-image" alt="Jimi Hendrix playing guitar left-handed on stage" loading="lazy" width="805" height="600" srcset="/content/images/size/w600/2022/10/jimmy-hendrix-left-handed.jpg 600w, /content/images/2022/10/jimmy-hendrix-left-handed.jpg 805w" sizes="(min-width: 720px) 720px"><figcaption>Jimi Hendrix playing a right-handed Stratocaster flipped for left-handed playing</figcaption></figure><p>Jimi Hendrix is widely regarded as one of the greatest guitarists of all time, regardless of handedness. He played left-handed, mainly on right-handed guitars that were turned upside down and restrung. He wrote with his right hand. Hendrix was born in Seattle, Washington in 1942 and got his first acoustic guitar in 1958, at age 15. He served in the US Army from 1961 to 1962 and then worked as a backing guitarist for acts including the Isley Brothers and Little Richard.</p><p>Hendrix moved to England in late 1966 after Chas Chandler of the Animals became his manager, and formed the Jimi Hendrix Experience there. His performance at the Monterey Pop Festival in 1967 made him famous in the United States. He helped popularize amplifier feedback as a musical sound and made heavy use of effects such as fuzz and the wah-wah pedal. Hendrix died in London in 1970 at age 27. The Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inducted the Jimi Hendrix Experience in 1992.</p><h2 id="paul-mccartney">Paul McCartney</h2><p><a href="https://www.paulmccartney.com/?ref=lefthanded.io">Paul McCartney</a> was born in Liverpool in 1942. As a teenager he traded a trumpet for a Zenith acoustic guitar and found it difficult to play right-handed. After seeing a poster for a Slim Whitman concert and noticing that Whitman played left-handed, he reversed the order of the strings and played it left-handed.</p><p>McCartney became the Beatles' bass player in 1961, after Stuart Sutcliffe left the band. He mainly used a Höfner 500/1 bass during the group's early years. He plays guitar and bass left-handed but plays drums right-handed.</p><h2 id="kurt-cobain">Kurt Cobain</h2><p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Cobain?ref=lefthanded.io">Kurt Cobain</a> was born in Aberdeen, Washington in 1967. He played guitar left-handed, although he was made to write right-handed. When Nirvana started, he was playing a right-handed Univox Hi-Flier that he flipped over and strung for left-handed playing.</p><p>Cobain said he had trouble finding good left-handed guitars. He came to prefer Japanese-made Fenders partly because they were widely available in left-handed versions, and his guitars included a 1965 Fender Jaguar and a 1969 Fender Mustang. For Nirvana's MTV Unplugged performance he played a right-handed 1959 Martin D-18E modified for left-handed playing. That guitar sold for more than $6 million at auction in 2020.</p><h2 id="tony-iommi">Tony Iommi</h2><p><a href="https://www.iommi.com/?ref=lefthanded.io">Tony Iommi</a> was born in Birmingham, England in 1948 and co-founded Black Sabbath in 1968. He is left-handed and plays left-handed, which means he frets with his right hand. At 17 he lost the tips of the middle and ring fingers of that hand in an accident at a sheet metal factory.</p><p>Iommi kept playing after a factory foreman played him a recording of Django Reinhardt, who also played with an injured fretting hand. He made thimbles for his fingertips from a melted plastic bottle, switched to lighter strings, and tuned his guitar down. He has said he tried switching to right-handed playing after the accident but did not have the patience for it. He is most associated with the Gibson SG and is often called the Godfather of Heavy Metal.</p><h2 id="albert-king">Albert King</h2><p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_King?ref=lefthanded.io">Albert King</a> was born in Indianola, Mississippi in 1923. He taught himself as a left-hander by turning a right-handed guitar upside down, and he never reversed the strings. He is most associated with the Gibson Flying V.</p><p>His 1967 Stax album <em>Born Under a Bad Sign</em> is his best-known record. Stevie Ray Vaughan often cited King as his greatest influence. King was inducted into the Blues Hall of Fame in 1983 and the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2013. He died in Memphis in 1992.</p><h2 id="dick-dale">Dick Dale</h2><p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Dale?ref=lefthanded.io">Dick Dale</a>, known as the King of the Surf Guitar, was born in Boston in 1937. Like Albert King, he played a right-handed guitar upside down without restringing it. He kept the same reversed stringing even after he got a left-handed guitar.</p><p>Dale worked with Leo Fender on louder amplifiers, which led to the Fender Showman. He recorded his version of "Misirlou" in 1962, and its use in the film <em>Pulp Fiction</em> revived his career in the 1990s. He died in 2019.</p><h2 id="other-notable-left-handed-guitarists">Other notable left-handed guitarists</h2><p>Chicago bluesman Otis Rush was left-handed and played left-handed with the low E string at the bottom, the same upside-down stringing used by Albert King and Dick Dale. Other guitarists documented as playing left-handed include Elliot Easton, Courtney Barnett, Omar Rodríguez-López, Eric Gales, Doyle Bramhall II, and Coco Montoya. <h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>Is Jimmy Page left-handed?</h3>
<p>No. Jimmy Page plays guitar right-handed and has been photographed signing with his right hand. The claim that he is a natural lefty is widely repeated but unverified. Read the evidence in <a href="/is-jimmy-page-left-handed/">is Jimmy Page left-handed?</a></p>

<h3>Is Eric Clapton left-handed?</h3>
<p>Eric Clapton plays guitar right-handed. He appears on some lists of left-handed guitarists, but we found no reliable source confirming that he is naturally left-handed.</p>

<h3>Did Jimi Hendrix play guitar left-handed?</h3>
<p>Yes. Jimi Hendrix played left-handed, mainly on right-handed guitars such as the Fender Stratocaster that he turned upside down and restrung.</p>

<h3>Who is the most famous left-handed guitarist?</h3>
<p>Jimi Hendrix is the most famous left-handed guitarist. Paul McCartney, Kurt Cobain, and Tony Iommi are the other best-known players who play or played left-handed.</p>
